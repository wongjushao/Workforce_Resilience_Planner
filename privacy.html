Privacy Policy

This draft explains how TalentPivot may collect, use, protect, and share information when organizations use the platform for workforce planning, skill analysis, redeployment, and upskilling decisions.

Information We Process

TalentPivot may process HR-uploaded employee data, role histories, resumes, profile details, manager inputs, learning records, skills, certifications, performance indicators, and workforce planning context provided by the customer organization.

Analytics and Recommendations

The platform may generate skill graphs, risk indicators, role recommendations, skill similarity scores, upskilling plans, and dashboard analytics. These outputs are decision-support materials and should be reviewed by authorized human decision-makers.

How Information Is Used

  • Identify workforce disruption and redeployment opportunities.
  • Normalize skills and compare them against role requirements.
  • Provide HR and employee dashboards.
  • Improve platform reliability, security, and user experience.

Cookies and Telemetry

TalentPivot may use essential cookies, session data, and basic telemetry to operate the service, maintain security, understand feature usage, and troubleshoot issues.

Sharing and Vendors

Information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, analytics, security, communications, or platform operations. Vendors should be bound by confidentiality, security, and limited-use obligations.

Security

A production deployment should use administrative safeguards, encryption, access controls, monitoring, audit logs, secure development practices, and least-privilege permissions to protect sensitive workforce data.

Retention

Customer data should be retained only as long as needed for the agreed business purpose, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security requirements. Customers may request deletion according to their contract and applicable law.

Employee Transparency

Organizations using TalentPivot should provide employees with appropriate notices about what data is processed, why it is used, who can access it, and how recommendations are reviewed.
